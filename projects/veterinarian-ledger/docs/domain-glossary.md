# Domain Glossary

- **Client**: Human owner responsible for one or more pets.
- **Pet**: Animal patient with species, breed, age, and medical profile.
- **Appointment**: Scheduled clinic slot.
- **Visit**: Actual consultation record with diagnosis and treatment notes.
- **Invoice**: Bill generated from services/products.
- **Payment**: Financial transaction against an invoice.
- **Ledger Entry**: Immutable accounting movement (charge, payment, refund, adjustment).
- **Balance**: Remaining amount due after payment allocations.
